# PBSG 2026-06-19 FAQ intake checklist

Source reviewed: `2026.06.19 FAQs.docx`

This checklist covers **all explicit question-form entries in the document**, plus the quoted question prompts in the later "difficult conversations" section.

## Legend
- [x] **Add verbatim** — keep as a standalone deterministic FAQ entry with the same user-facing question intent.
- [~] **Add in changed form** — support the topic, but merge / rename / narrow it into a safer FAQ bucket.
- [ ] **Do not add** — keep out of the deterministic FAQ layer because it is too routing-sensitive, action-specific, or urgency-sensitive.

## Scope note
This checklist **does not list non-question prompts** such as:
- “Just tell me what to do.”
- “I don’t trust LAB/PDO.”
- “I am in danger now.”
- “I want to hurt myself.”
- “I need help with shelter, food, or finances.”
- “I registered for a legal talk and have questions about CPD points or the recording of the talk.”

Those should be handled outside the deterministic FAQ matcher.
